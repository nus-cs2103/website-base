# {{ title }}

<box type="warning" seamless>

This scheme is being tried for the first time and subject to approval at various admin layers.
</box>

**Objective:** To give CS2103/T students the option to earn 2 extra units of course credits under CP3108A/B, for doing some of the optional (but strongly recommended) CS2103/T work.

**Process:**

* Student who opt-in enroll in CP3108A/B concurrently with CS2103/T.
* Enrolled students do the CS2103/T tasks that can be counted for CP3108A/B. The teaching team monitors and grade the work.
* Those who completes the work get 2 Units of course credit under CP3108A/B.

<box type="info" seamless>

CP3108A (2 Units) and CP3108B (4 Units) are [Independent Study Courses (ISC)](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/programmes/ug/project/isc/), graded `CS`/`CU`.
</box>

**Eligibility:**

* **If you have not done CP3108A or CP3108B**, you can take this option to earn credit under CP3108A.
* **If you have done CP3108A before but not CP3108B**, you can enroll to 'upgrade' your CP3108A to CP3108B, adding two more Units of course credit.
* **If you have done CP3108B already**, you are not eligible to take this option.

**Workload:**

Period | Task | If taking CS2103/T only
-------|------|--------------
W1-W5 | Do at least 80% of the [Git-Mastery](https://git-mastery.org/lessons/index.html#/cs2103) exercises that are marked as compulsory, and ensure progress tracking feature is enabled. | Optional,<br>strongly recommended
W3-W4 | Complete parts 1-4 and at least half of part 5 of the [JavaFX tutorial]({{ baseUrl }}/admin/ip-w3.html#6-optional-get-a-head-start-on-javafx). | first 4 are necessary for the iP anyway, 5th is optional
W5-W6 | Do at least 4 iP increments marked as <span class="badge rounded-pill bg-secondary text-light">optional</span> ([Examples]({{ baseUrl }}/admin/ip-w6.html#2-add-increments-a-bettergui-a-personality-a-moreerrorhandling-a-moretesting)).<br> {{ icon_tip }} You may use AI to do these. | At least 2 optional increments are 'required'
W6-W7 | Do 2 out of 3 [AB3 tutorials]({{ baseUrl }}/admin/tp-w6.html#4-get-familiar-with-the-codebase) | 1 required, the rest optional
W2-W10 | Learn basics tools Gradle, Checkstyle, Codecov, Codex, PlantUML, an IDE | Most are necessry for the course projects anyway

Note: The #r#CP3108A/B deadline for each task## is the same as its deadline for CS2103/T.

**Benefits** of taking this option:

* You earn 2 extra course credits. If you anticipate your workload will be heavy this semester, this allows you to drop another 4-unit course to reduce the total load.
* Most of the work required for CP3108A/B is 'necessary' (if not compulsory) for CS2103/T anyway. %%For example, you need to do the JavaFX tutorial to learn JavaFX before you use JavaFX in the two projects.%% So, most of the work listed above are not really "extra" work.

**Cautions:**

* Your actual workload will increase slightly if you don't drop another course to make room.<br>
  So, if you are overloaded already, and you do not wish to drop any of those courses, it may be better not to take this option.
* If you don't complete the required work, you will get a `CU` grade.

**Grading:** Will be done by the CS2103/T teaching team, as part of CS2103/T. No extra report submission is needed.

**Enrolling:**

1. Submit the `Extra 2 Units Under CP3108A -- Opt in/out Form`. #r#Deadline: Week 1 Wednesday##
1. Wait till we confirm enrollment status (don't drop any courses until your enrollment is confirmed).

{{ faq }} **Can I opt in now and withdraw later?**<br>
**A:** Yes, you need to write to UG office with the request, before the normal deadline with dropping courses.

{{ faq }} **Can I do the work this semester and claim CP3108A/B credit in a later semester?**<br>
**A:** No, this special arrangement is only if you do the two courses in parallel.
